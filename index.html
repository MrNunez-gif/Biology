📖 Mission Guide

How to play: Each room has one challenge. Solve it, submit your answer, and receive the code for the next room.

Decision process: Read → Choose → Defend → Adapt → Analyze → Compare → Reflect.

Team language: “We chose this because…” • “Our evidence suggests…” • “This decision matters because…”

Teacher tip: Students can work in teams and enter the code together. The codes can also be printed and placed around the classroom as physical locks.
